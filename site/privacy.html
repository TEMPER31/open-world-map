Privacy & disclaimer

Watching places, not people

Open World Map has no accounts and no ads. It runs no visitor-statistics or tracking scripts, sets no tracking or advertising cookies and does nothing to recognise your device. This page lists every outside service your browser contacts, and when.

What is stored on your device

The only thing the site stores is your map preferences, in your browser's local storage under the key owm:prefs:v1: units, the Dark or Light basemap, the chosen field and overlays, playback settings and the 24 h / 7 d events choice. They never leave your device. Events, street photos and satellite are not remembered: they start off every time you open the map, so their services are contacted only after you turn them on. Event results and photo details are kept in memory only while the map is open.

Place search runs entirely in your browser. The list of places is downloaded from this site the first time you use the search box or click the map, and what you type is not sent anywhere and not stored. The map position in the address bar moves to the place you pick, as it does whenever the map moves.

What every service receives

Any server your browser contacts receives your IP address and the normal request details, including your browser's user agent. The site sets the referrer policy strict-origin-when-cross-origin, so other sites see at most this site's address, never the page you were on. The map position after the # in the address is never sent. Mapillary photo images are requested with no referrer at all.

Behind the scenes

The forecast files are built by a scheduled job on GitHub Actions, not by your browser. That job downloads NOAA GFS from NOAA's NOMADS server or its AWS Open Data mirror and uses the Open-Meteo Air Quality and Forecast APIs. You receive only the finished files, from GitHub Pages.

Disclaimer

  • The weather on this map is a computer model forecast on a grid of about 50 km. Mountains, coasts and cities can differ from the grid value, and forecasts become less certain beyond about 3 days.
  • It is not an official warning service. For decisions about safety, use your national weather service and local authorities.
  • Event, alert and storm information comes from third-party feeds and can be delayed, incomplete or withdrawn. Check the source.
