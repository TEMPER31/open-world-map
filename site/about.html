About

A free world map of weather and natural events

Open World Map is a free, open-source, non-commercial world map. It shows a 7-day weather forecast, air quality, natural events and street-level photos on one shared timeline.

Six weather fields (wind with moving particles, temperature, precipitation, clouds, pressure and air quality) come from global forecast models and are rebuilt four times a day. Earthquakes, wildfires, volcanoes, storms and disaster alerts come from public agency feeds, and street-level photos from Mapillary's open community imagery. The Sources & licences page lists every one.

Watching places, not people

There are no accounts, no ads, no visitor statistics and no tracking cookies. Your settings stay in your own browser. Optional layers contact their services only while they are on. The Privacy & disclaimer page lists every service your browser contacts, and when.

Free and non-commercial

The map is free to use and will stay that way: no ads, no paid features and no sponsorships. It is a static site on GitHub Pages; GitHub Actions rebuilds the forecast data four times a day.

Honest about what it shows

  • Each layer shows its source, when it was updated and its grid size.
  • Weather values are model forecasts on a grid of about 50 km, not observations or official warnings. The Data page explains the limits.
  • Features that are not built yet say so: search, place names and data downloads are marked “Coming soon”.

Open source

The code is published under the MIT licence; data and imagery keep their own licences. Source code, history and the project's documentation are on GitHub: github.com/TEMPER31/open-world-map.
