Data

What the map shows, and its limits

The weather on the map is a computer forecast on a global grid. This page says where each layer comes from, how often it is updated, how fine its grid is and where it is known to be off.

Weather forecast

  • Source: NOAA/NCEP Global Forecast System (GFS), 0.25° model output.
  • Range: 137 frames per run: hourly to +5 days (f000–f120), then every 3 hours to +7 days (f123–f168). Forecasts become less certain beyond about 3 days; the timeline marks that part.
  • Updates: usually four times a day, after each GFS cycle (00, 06, 12 and 18 UTC) is complete. A run's files appear about 4½ hours after its cycle; the map's header shows when the data was built. If an update fails, the map keeps the previous run and shows a “may be out of date” banner.
  • Grids on the map: temperature and pressure on a 0.5° grid (about 50 km) with NOAA's exact values at the grid points; wind on a 0.75° grid; clouds and precipitation as 0.5° area averages (about 50 km across).
  • Precipitation is the mean rate over the past hour, or the past 3 hours after +5 days.
  • History: every run's forecast for 20 cities, every 6 hours to +7 days, is kept on this site at data/archive/ (JSON) for a future accuracy page.

Air quality

  • Source: CAMS global forecast (Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service) via the Open-Meteo Air Quality API, CC BY 4.0.
  • Grid: 4° (3,870 points), smoothed between points in your browser.
  • Updates: about twice a day. The CAMS forecast ends about 4½ days after its run; after that the map says “Air quality forecast ends” with the date.
  • Scale: PM2.5 colours and categories use the US EPA bands revised in 2024, applied to hourly values. The AQI number is the 24-hour value.

Events, photos and satellite

  • Events: USGS earthquakes (M2.5+, past 7 days), NASA EONET wildfires, volcanoes and severe storms, GDACS disaster alerts, and NOAA National Hurricane Center forecast tracks and cones, which follow the timeline. Your browser fetches a feed only while its toggle is on, at most every 10 minutes.
  • Street photos: Mapillary community photos (CC BY-SA 4.0), each with its capture date and creator.
  • Satellite basemap: NASA GIBS true colour from the previous day at world and regional zoom, EOX Sentinel-2 cloudless 2025 from regional zoom, and USGS aerial imagery at street zoom in the contiguous US.

Place search and place names

  • Source: GeoNames towns and cities with more than 5,000 people, or that are seats of government (cities5000), with their state or province, CC BY 4.0. About 70,000 places.
  • In your browser: the place list (about 1.2 MB compressed) loads from this site the first time you use search or click the map. Typing a place or “lat, lon” is searched on your device.
  • Place names on a clicked point are the nearest town or city in the list, within 100 km, with its distance. They are not addresses, and smaller villages are not in the list. With nothing within 100 km, only the coordinates are shown.

Known limits

  • Temperature is stored on a 0.5° grid, so at a city it can read colder than the full 0.25° model. Across one full week of forecast for 20 cities (the 18Z run on 10 Nov 2026), most were within about 0.6 °C on average, but Denver averaged 2.4 °C cold (up to 4.9), Cairo 1.8, Reykjavík 1.5 and Delhi 1.0. Nairobi read 0.9 °C warm.
  • Wind is stored on a 0.75° grid. In the same week most cities were within 3 km/h on average, but Lagos averaged 5 km/h low and London about 4 km/h high, and single hours at coastal cities were off by up to 10–19 km/h.
  • City air quality can be well off because the CAMS grid used is 4°. In checks so far, Delhi has read 16–103 µg/m³ low and Denver 4–23 µg/m³ low against the CAMS value at the city.
  • Mountains, coasts and cities can differ from the grid value at a point. Model values are not observations.
  • When live data is unavailable, the map shows clearly labelled sample data instead of a forecast.
  • The place panel's quick view takes its 48-hour and 7-day temperature and wind from the coarser summary file behind the forecast chart (its grid and time steps are shown with the rows). Rain by day is not in that file, so the 7-day rows leave it out.
